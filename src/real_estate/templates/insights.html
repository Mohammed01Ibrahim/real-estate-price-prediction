06 / MODEL INTERPRETATION

Which inputs the selected pipeline relied on

These are the saved permutation scores from the original validation split used for model selection. They are not permutation scores recomputed on the final test set.

Loading validation permutation scores…

Validation permutation importance

Mean increase in MAE after shuffling an input · USD

A larger positive increase means prediction error grew when that input was shuffled. Error bars show the standard deviation across repeats. Near-zero or negative scores offer little evidence of reliable importance on this split.

Permutation importance describes a fitted pipeline's predictive reliance. Correlated inputs may share importance, and shuffling can create unrealistic combinations. Scores depend on the sample and error metric; they do not identify causal drivers or effects on a property's value.

Use this dashboard in context

These are historical asking-price observations and an imperfect portfolio model. Dataset coverage, unconfirmed source semantics, missing details, and extreme prices limit interpretation. The dashboard does not establish present-day market values or uncertainty intervals.
